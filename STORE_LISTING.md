## Full Description (recommended: 700–900 words; keyword-rich, scannable)

**TextHider** is the fastest way to hide, censor, or mask any text on any webpage in Chrome.

Select text on any site, right-click, choose a mask style, and it's hidden instantly. Word-length aware — a 5-letter word becomes `*****`, preserving the visual rhythm of the page. **Zero data collected. Free forever.**

---

### ✦ Why People Use TextHider

- **Screenshots** — hide names, emails, account numbers, or any sensitive data before sharing
- **Screen sharing** — protect private info during Zoom, Teams, or Google Meet sessions
- **Client demos** — present your work without exposing other clients' confidential details
- **Tutorials & guides** — create how-to content without revealing personal information
- **Financial pages** — mask balances, PINs, and account numbers while browsing banking sites
- **Spoiler blocking** — hide spoiler text on news, Reddit, or social media

---

### ✦ How to Use

1. Select any text on a webpage with your mouse
2. Right-click → choose **TextHider** → pick your mask style
3. The text is replaced instantly — click it again to reveal or re-hide

Keyboard shortcut: **Ctrl+Shift+H** (Cmd+Shift+H on Mac)

---

### ✦ Built-in Mask Presets

8 presets built in, all word-length aware:

| Symbol | Name |
|--------|------|
| `.` | Dots |
| `*` | Stars |
| `#` | Hash |
| `█` | Redact (full block) |
| `?` | Question marks |
| ❤️ | Love |
| 🔥 | Fire |
| 🔒 | Lock |

**Custom masks:** Use any character, emoji, or symbol. Save it as a permanent preset.

---

### ✦ Advanced Features

**🔍 Find & Mask** — Type any word in the popup to see a live match count, then mask all occurrences on the page at once.

**↩ Undo Last** — Instantly restore the last group of hidden text with one click.

**⏱ Peek Mode** — Temporarily reveal all hidden text for 5 seconds, then it auto re-hides.

**🤖 Auto-hide Filters** — Add words or phrases in Settings that are automatically masked on every page load across all sites.

**🎨 6 Highlight Themes** — Crimson, Ocean Blue, Forest Green, Gold, Purple, Monochrome, and No Highlight (invisible with dotted underline).

**🔢 Badge Counter** — The toolbar icon shows how many items are hidden on the current page.

**👁 Reveal All / Re-hide All** — Reveal or re-mask everything on the page in one click.

**📋 Preset Manager** — Drag to reorder, edit labels, add/remove presets from the full Options page.

**📤 Export / Import Presets** — Back up or share your custom presets as JSON.

---

### ✦ Privacy — Zero Data Collection

- The text you hide is **never sent anywhere**
- No browsing history is accessed or stored
- No analytics, no trackers, no ads, no third-party SDKs
- Preferences (preset labels and mask symbols only) stored locally via Chrome sync storage
- No account or sign-in required — ever
- Full privacy policy: https://shariarf39.github.io/TextHider-Extention/privacy.html

---

### ✦ Permissions Used

- **contextMenus** — show the right-click hide menu
- **storage** — save your presets and settings locally
- **activeTab** — interact with the page when you click the toolbar icon
- **scripting** — run the masking logic in response to your actions

No broad host permissions. No access to passwords or form data.
