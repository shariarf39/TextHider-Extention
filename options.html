🤖 Auto-hide Filters

Words and phrases masked automatically every time a page loads. Useful for hiding recurring sensitive terms like names, account numbers, or passwords across all sites.

⚠️ Reset

Reset all presets and settings to factory defaults. This cannot be undone.

📦 Export & Import Presets

Back up your custom presets as a JSON file, or restore from a previous backup.

⌨️ Keyboard Shortcut

Press Ctrl+Shift+H (or ⌘+Shift+M on Mac) to instantly hide the selected text using your last used mask.

To change the shortcut: go to chrome://extensions/shortcuts
